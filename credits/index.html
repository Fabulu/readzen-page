DILA Person Authority Database — the Zen master roster

The master profiles and the lineage graph use factual data — authority identifiers, dates, school affiliations, and teacher/student relations — checked against, and in places sourced from, the Person Authority Database of the Dharma Drum Institute of Liberal Arts. It is an authority record of some 49,000 figures from Chinese Buddhist biographies and temple gazetteers.
