Lingyin Tuigeng Ning 靈隱退耕德寧

School: Linji | Teacher: Wuzhun Shifan

Southern Song Linji (Yangqi branch) master, dharma name Dening and style Tuigeng ('Retired to Ploughing'); dharma heir of Wuzhun Shifan of Mt. Jing and abbot of Lingyin monastery at Hangzhou. He has a 法嗣 heading of his own in the Xu chuandeng lu, so his line continued.
