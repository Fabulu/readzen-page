Changlu Yingfu 長蘆應夫

School: Yunmen | Teacher: Tianyi Yihuai | Students: Changlu Zongze, Xuedou Daorong

Zhenzhou Changlu Yingfu, of the Jiang clan of Chuzhou (Anhui). Dharma heir of Tianyi Yihuai (992-1064), 11th generation after Huineng. Ordained under Chengtai at Baoning monastery in Jiangning, then studied under Tianyi Yihuai and inherited the Yunmen essentials. Held forth at Changlu with 25 recorded dharma heirs including Changlu Zongze (author of the Chanyuan Qinggui) and Xuedou Daorong.
