Xianglin Benzhen 象林本真

School: Linji | 1620–1691 | Teacher: Tieguan Xingneng 鐵關行能 | Students: Xixin Zhaoshui

Of Suizhou, Qiu clan; born 1620. Abbot of Baiyun monastery at Qizhou, where he is styled Xianglin. A dharma heir of Tieguan Xingneng. His recorded sayings, the Baiyun Xianglin Benzhen chanshi yulu, contain his 行實, which records that he died sitting cross-legged in the summer of 1691, aged 72.
