Jie'an Yongzhen Fuliang 杭州靈隱介菴用真輔良

School: Linji | d. 1371 | Teacher: Xiaoyin Daxin

Fuliang, styled Jie'an, a Suzhou man of the Fan family, dharma-heir of Xiaoyin Daxin and abbot of Lingyin monastery in Hangzhou. Born in 1317 under the Yuan, he died in 1371 in the early Ming aged 55 with forty years in the robe.
