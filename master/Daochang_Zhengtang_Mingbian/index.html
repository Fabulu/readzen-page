Daochang Zhengtang Mingbian 道場正堂明辯

School: Linji | 1085–1157 | Teacher: Foyan Qingyuan

Song-dynasty Chan master of the Linji/Yangqi line, dharma-heir of Longmen Foyan Qingyuan. A native of Huzhou, surname Yu, he taught at the Daochang monastery in Anji (Huzhou) and was known by the style Zhengtang.
