Zhaoqing Fayin / Zhaoqing Daokuang 招慶法因

School: Yunmen | Teacher: Changqing Huileng | Students: Zongxian Minghui, Jingshan Daoqin

Five Dynasties Min monk, surname Li, from Zhouzhou. Entered Min, trained under Yishan, silently transmitted the mind-source. Followed Changqing Huileng in his abbacy at Zhaoqing yuan, Quanzhou; when Huileng was summoned by King Wang Shenzhi to Changle, he succeeded him as abbot at Zhaoqing, given the title 'Fayin Dashi' by the Min king. Dates unrecorded.
