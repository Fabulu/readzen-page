Zhengfa Ximing 正法希明

School: Linji | Teacher: Qingyuan Weixin

成都府正法希明禪師, a native of 漢州, dharma heir of Qingyuan Weixin in the Linji Huanglong branch. His recorded 解制上堂 begins 「林葉紛紛落，乾坤報早秋。分明西祖意，何用更馳求」and argues that once the source is fathomed, 「動靜施為，經行坐臥，頭頭合道，念念朝宗」— walking, sitting and every act accord with the Way; without it, 「百劫熏功，千生鍊行」are wasted effort.
