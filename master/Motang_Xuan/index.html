Motang Xuan 默堂宣

School: Linji | Teacher: Baoyue Tan | Students: Ji'an Zuo

Chan master of Tianning monastery in Jiaxing prefecture. A dharma heir of Baoyue Tan. The Jingshi diru ji records that he first went to Chushan Shaoqi — Baoyue's dharma-brother — and 'at once tallied with the subtle purport', before receiving Baoyue's seal: another case where the first teacher is not the transmitting one. Only his dharma character 宣 is attested; I have not expanded it.
