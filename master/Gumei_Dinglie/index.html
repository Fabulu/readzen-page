Gumei Dinglie 古梅定冽

School: Linji | Teacher: Huishan Zhihai | Students: Qizong Zhen, Tianyi Juxiang

Of Shaoling, surname Wang. He studied at the county school and, gripped by the greatness of birth-and-death, was tonsured and ordained. He relied on Huishan Zhihai and worked under him for fifteen years. He went out to head Guanghua on Mt. Furong, and on the twenty-first day of the ninth month of 1669 succeeded to the seat of Miyin monastery on Mt. Dawei — the great Guiyang site. His heir was Tianyi Juxiang.
