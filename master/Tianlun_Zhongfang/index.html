Tianlun Zhongfang 江寧府保寧仲方天倫禪師

School: Linji | Teacher: Huiji Yuanxi

Zhongfang Tianlun, Chan master of Baoning in Jiangning, a dharma heir of Huiji Yuanxi of Jingshan in the Linji line, 大鑑下第二十一世 (Yuan dynasty). He moved to Foyan in Mingzhou; when Xiaoyin Daxin was residing at Longxiang he invited Tianlun to share the seat and preach, and the censor Tulu then recommended him as abbot of Baoning.
