Letan Hongying 泐潭洪英

School: Linji | 1012–1070 | Teacher: Huanglong Huinan | Students: Nanyue Falun Qitian

Northern Song Linji master of the Huanglong branch (1012-1070), dharma heir of Huanglong Huinan and abbot at Letan in Longxing, Jiangxi. Known also as 'Ying of Shaowu' after his home prefecture; seven dharma heirs are recorded.
