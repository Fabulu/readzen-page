Baizhang Yuansu 百丈元肅

School: Linji | Teacher: Huanglong Huinan | Students: Jiuxian Qifu

Song Linji master of the Huanglong line at Baizhang in Longxing, an heir of Huanglong Huinan. Asked to reveal the meaning of the coming from the West, he answered 「泥牛吞巨浪」 (the clay ox swallows the great waves).
