Huangbo Yi'an Yue 瑞州黃檗一菴月

School: Linji | d. 1655 | Teacher: Ruoan Tongwen

Yi'an Yue of Huangbo in Ruizhou, a native of Yangzhou surnamed Tu and a dharma-heir of Ruo'an Tongwen in the Linji-Yangqi line. He headed the ancestral Huangbo mountain and died in 1655, a few months before his teacher, leaving three heirs.
