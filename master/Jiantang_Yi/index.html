Jiantang Yi 鑑堂一

School: Linji | Teacher: Zhufeng Huanmin

A Qing Linji master, dharma heir of Zhufeng Huanmin (1638-1707) and thirty-first generation below Nanyue. He opened the dharma at Changle Chan monastery. Only the first character of his dharma name (一) is recorded anywhere, and it is not expanded in any source I could reach. His recorded sayings survive in one fascicle, compiled by his heirs Mingman and Minghui. No dates.
