Jingshan Bao 荊山寶

School: Linji | Teacher: Wunian You | Students: Tieniu Deyuan

A monk of Zhugong (Jiangling). He is remembered in a single exchange: coming to Wunian You, he was asked, 'Trekking county to county, roaming prefecture to prefecture — what in the end is it all for?' He said, 'The great matter of birth-and-death. I ask the master to ferry me across.' Wunian said, 'Are you a man of Jingzhou, then?' He stands in the Juyun branch's reconstructed line of Dahui Zonggao, between Wunian You above him and Tieniu Deyuan below.
