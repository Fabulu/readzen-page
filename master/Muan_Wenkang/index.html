Mu'an Wenkang 穆菴文康

School: Linji | Teacher: Liao'an Qingyu

A Yuan Linji monk of Cixi and heir of Liao'an Qingyu, whose recorded sayings he helped edit (fascicle 2). He was installed at Mingyan Dafan on Mount Tiantai in 1357, moved to Longhua Baosheng in Hangzhou in 1364, and in 1374, under the new Ming, took Jinshan Longyou at Zhenjiang. He left a one-fascicle record of his own; his heir was Qingyi. His dates are not recorded.
