Xixin Zhaoshui 洗心照水

School: Linji | Teacher: Xianglin Benzhen

Linji master of Xiangyan, thirty-seventh generation below Nanyue. Dharma heir of Xianglin Zhen (象林本真, 1620–1691). He re-cut and reissued his teacher's recorded sayings and also re-edited the collected writings of Zhengjue Runguang Ze — the acts of a literary executor to his own line. His own 語錄 carries a 行實.
