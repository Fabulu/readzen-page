Chengtian Chuanzong 承天傳宗

School: Yunmen | Teacher: Xuedou Chongxian

Song Yunmen-school master of Chengtian in Quanzhou (also recorded at Hangzhou), an heir of Xuedou Chongxian. When a monk shouted at him he said 「臨濟兒孫」 — a child and grandchild of Linji; when the monk shouted again, he struck him.
