Pufu Zhuo 樸夫拙

d. 1781 | Teacher: Guyun Pei

Lay surname Li. He was tonsured by Dongxu Xinguang and received the dharma from Guyun Pei. He held Yongzhen at Jianxi, Xiting at Bailu and Caoan at Qingyun, and gave the precepts at Qingyun in Fengcheng, Yuantong in Fuliang and Tianzhu in Chongren. He was still alive in 1737, aged fifty-six. His record runs to six fascicles.
