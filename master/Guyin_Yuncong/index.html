Guyin Yuncong 谷隱蘊聰

School: Linji | 965–1032 | Teacher: Shoushan Xingnian | Students: Longhua Qiyue, Jinshan Tanying

Yuncong of Guyin (965–1032), styled Cizhao and also known as Shimen Cong, surnamed Zhang of Nanhai. He first trained under Baizhang Daochang before awakening under Shoushan Xingnian, whose heir he became; he headed Shimen shan in Xiangzhou from 1006 and Taiping Xingguo cloister on Guyin shan from 1020, leaving the Fengyan ji and some ten heirs.
