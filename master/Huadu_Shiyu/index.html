Huadu Shiyu 杭州西興化度院師郁悟真

School: Shitou | Teacher: Xuefeng Yicun

Five Dynasties Chan master from Quanzhou (Putian), dharma-heir of Xuefeng Yicun, from whom he received the mind-seal. His teaching flourished in the Hangzhou-Yue region and he settled at the Huadu cloister in Xixing. Titled Wuzhen.
