Chengxin Shengzong 稱心省倧

School: Yunmen | Teacher: Xuedou Chongxian

Song Yunmen-school master of Chengxin in Yuezhou, an heir of Xuedou Chongxian. Holding up his staff he asked which is the seed of buddhahood and which the condition, warning that having met Old Sakyamuni here one should not then buy straw sandals and go wandering.
