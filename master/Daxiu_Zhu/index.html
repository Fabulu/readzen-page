Daxiu Zhu 大休珠

School: Caodong | 1615–1656 | Teacher: Jinian Jingxian

A Qing Caodong master and heir of Xiangtian Jinian Jingxian, whose recorded sayings he himself edited (fascicles 2 and 3). He held Nanhua at Caoxi, Bailian and Dingyin at Zuili, Miyin in Huzhou and Tianhua at Yuecheng, and left a twelve-fascicle record of his own. He died on the 15th of the 8th month of 1656, aged only 42, with twenty-three years in the robe.
