Liangshan Huan 潭州梁山懽

School: Linji | Teacher: Qingyuan Weixin

Northern Song Chan master of the Linji/Huanglong line who taught at Liangshan in Tanzhou (Hunan). Dharma-heir of Qingyuan Weixin, himself an heir of Huanglong Huitang Zuxin.
