Sanjiao Zhisong 三交智嵩

School: Linji | Teacher: Shoushan Xingnian

Northern Song Linji master at Chengtian cloister in Bingzhou (Taiyuan), also known as Tangming; dharma heir of Shoushan Xingnian and thus a dharma brother of Fenyang Shanzhao. His heir Fushan Fayuan became one of the notable teachers of the next generation.
