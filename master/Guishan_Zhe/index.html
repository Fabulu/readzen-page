Guishan Zhe 大溈慕喆

School: Linji | d. 1095 | Teacher: Cuiyan Kezhen | Students: Letan Jingxiang, Guangxiao Huilan, Zhihai Purong Daoping

Muzhe of Dawei (Guishan) in Tanzhou, surnamed Wen of Linchuan, a dharma-heir of Cuiyan Kezhen in the Linji line. He headed Yuelu, Huiguang and Dawei, and in 1094 was summoned to court, granted the purple robe and the title Zhenru, and appointed to Zhihai cloister at the Great Xiangguo monastery; he died in 1095. His comments on old cases are widely quoted in later anthologies.
