Lingzhao Zhenjue 杭州龍華寺靈照真覺

School: Shitou | d. 947 | Teacher: Xuefeng Yicun

A Korean-born (高麗) monk of the Five Dynasties who travelled in Min and Yue and became a dharma-heir of Xuefeng Yicun. He taught at Longhua monastery in Hangzhou and was titled Zhenjue dashi (真覺大師); also known as Zhao buna (照布衲) and Qiyun heshang. Died 947, aged 78.
