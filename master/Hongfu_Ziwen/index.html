Hongfu Ziwen 洪福子文

School: Linji | Teacher: Yuanwu Keqin

台州洪福子文禪師, a Southern Song Linji master of the Yangqi branch, 15th generation below Nanyue and a dharma heir of Yuanwu Keqin — listed among his heirs alongside Dahui Zonggao and Huqiu Shaolong. His recorded 上堂 turns on the wild-fox kōan: 「不昧不落作麼會？會得依前墮野狐。一夜涼風生畫角，滿船明月泛江湖。」
