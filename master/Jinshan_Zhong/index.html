Jinshan Zhong 鎮江金山佛鑑惟仲

School: Yunmen | Teacher: Foguo Weibai

Weizhong of Jinshan at Zhenjiang, titled Fojian, a native of Tingzhou and a dharma-heir of Fayun Weibai — the Yunmen master who compiled the Jianzhong Jingguo Continued Lamp Record. He awakened on the 'cypress in the courtyard' case at Guishan, attended Weibai at Fayun in the Eastern Capital, and on the recommendation of chief councillor Zeng Bu opened the hall at Jinshan in 1101.
