Wunian You 無念有

School: Linji | d. 1534 | Teacher: Eryang Qin | Students: Jingshan Bao

From Hanzhou in Shu (Chengdu prefecture); dharma heir of Eryang Yuanqin. He died in 1534. His heir was Jingshan Huaibao of Zhugong.
