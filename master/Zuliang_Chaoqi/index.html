Zuliang Chaoqi 祖亮超啟

School: Linji | Teacher: Lingji Xingguan

Linji master, dharma heir of Lingji Xingguan. A preface to his own record of sayings calls him 'my dharma-brother Zuliang, who awakened and verified in his prime, entering in person the room of the old man Lingji at Yangfen.' He left a record of sayings in two fascicles.
