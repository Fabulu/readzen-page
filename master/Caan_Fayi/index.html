Ca'an Fayi 草菴法義

School: Caodong | Teacher: Caoshan Benji

Tang/Five Dynasties Caodong master of Cao'an in Huazhou, an heir of Caoshan Benji. Asked how a student advances when 'intending misses and stirring a thought goes wrong', he answered that the miss is already made.
