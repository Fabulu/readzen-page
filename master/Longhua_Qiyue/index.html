Longhua Qiyue 龍華齊岳

School: Linji | Teacher: Guyin Yuncong | Students: Wushan Jingduan

A Northern Song Linji master, dharma heir of Guyin Yuncong (Guyin Cong). The Xu chuandeng lu files him under 'the heirs of Chan master Guyin Cong' and records his exchanges; Xiyu Jingduan and Cuiyan Xianchou came from his gate. No dates survive.
