Shanquan Huitai 常州善權慧泰禪師

School: Yunmen | Teacher: Foyin Liaoyuan

Chan master of Shanquan in Changzhou, a dharma heir of Yunju Yuan in the Yunmen line. His recorded sermon distinguishes superior students who leap through at a word from middling and lesser ones who must clarify the mind-ground by degrees.
