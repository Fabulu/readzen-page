Qiaoyin Wuyi 樵隱悟逸

School: Linji | d. 1334 | Teacher: Jue'an Kexiang

Linji master, of the Nie family of Huai'an, abbot of Xuefeng at Fuzhou. He was the dharma heir of Jue'an Kexiang, on whose death he wrote: 'In the autumn of the gengyin year my late master, the venerable Jue'an, passed away at Censhan in Hangzhou; on hearing the news of his death I made this.' He also mourned Xueyan Zuqin of Yangshan. He died about 1334.
