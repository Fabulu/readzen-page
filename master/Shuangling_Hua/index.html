Shuangling Hua 隆興府雙嶺化禪師

School: Linji | Teacher: Huitang Zuxin

Chan master of Shuangling in Longxing (Jiangxi), a dharma heir of Huanglong Zuxin, 臨濟宗 黃龍派, 南嶽下十三世. His sermon: 'Green bamboo and yellow blossoms are no outer realm; white cloud and bright moon reveal the whole real. Every single thing is a possession of my house — yet pick one up offhand and it still is not dust.'
