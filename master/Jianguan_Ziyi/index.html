Jianguan Ziyi 劍關子益

School: Linji | d. 1267 | Teacher: Wuzhun Shifan

Of Jianzhou in Sichuan, zi Jianguan. A dharma heir of Wuzhun Shifan. He headed Xinghua monastery in Longxing prefecture from 1239, then moved to Yunyan Shouning, and in 1265 took Xichan Yishan Changqing monastery in Fuzhou. He died in 1267.
