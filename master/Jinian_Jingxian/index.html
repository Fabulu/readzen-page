Jinian Jingxian 象田即念淨現

School: Caodong | Teacher: Shiyu Mingfang | Students: Daxiu Zhu

A Qing Caodong master of Guyu, abbot of Xiangtian in Yue, and an heir of Shiyu Mingfang. His four-fascicle recorded sayings survive. He appears in his teacher Shiyu's record attending him in his last illness, asking the old question 'there is one who is not sick — who is he?' His own heir was Daxiu Jingzhu of Nanhua at Caoxi.
