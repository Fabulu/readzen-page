Jiufeng Jianshao 九峰鑑韶

School: Yunmen | Teacher: Letan Huaicheng | Students: Damei Ying

Northern Song Yunmen-school monk, abbot of Jiufeng in Mingzhou (also given as Hangzhou Lin'an Jiufeng Jingtu). Dharma heir of Letan Huaicheng, 11th generation from Huineng. Wrote a petition urging Dajue Huailian to accept the abbacy of Ayuwang monastery. Dharma heir: Zujing Fayin.
