Jiuxian Yuxian 酒仙遇賢

922–1009 | Teacher: Longhua Yanqiu

The 'Wine Immortal' (酒仙), a Northern Song eccentric master from Changzhou in Gusu (Suzhou), surname Lin, whose mother is said to have conceived him after dreaming she swallowed a great pearl. Dharma-heir of Longhua Yanqiu. Died 1009, aged 88.
