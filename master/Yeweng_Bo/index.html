Yeweng Bo 冶翁伯

School: Linji | Teacher: Guquan Qing

樂清雁山羅漢寺冶翁伯禪師 (Chan Master Yeweng Bo of Luohan Monastery, Mt. Yandang, Yueqing). Late Ming/Qing Linji-school monk, dharma-heir of 古泉清 (Guquan Qing, abbot of 永嘉護國寺), who was in turn heir of 通奇/林野奇 (Tong Qi / Linye Qi, 1596-1652), a disciple of 密雲圓悟 (Miyun Yuanwu). Had at least one recorded dharma-heir of his own, 斯準衡 (per DILA).
