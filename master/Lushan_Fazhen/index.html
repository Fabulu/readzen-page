Lushan Fazhen 蘆山無相法真

School: Linji | Teacher: Guangxiao Huilan

Northern Song Linji master styled Wuxiang ('No-Marks') at Lushan in Mingzhou; dharma heir of Guangxiao Huilan, the eccentric known as the 'Daoist of Biluo' who wrote the names of the seven buddhas on his loincloth and was nicknamed 'Lan of the Cloth Trousers'.
