Baizhao Tonghui Gui 白兆山通慧珪

School: Yunmen | Teacher: Yuantong Faxiu

Song Yunmen-school master of Mount Baizhao in Anzhou, heir of Fayun Fashiu of the Eastern Capital. His sermons press the point that the self-nature is already complete in ordinary walking, sitting and lying.
