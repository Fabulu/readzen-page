Guangxiao Huijue 楊州光孝慧覺

School: Hongzhou | Teacher: Zhaozhou Congshen

Huijue of Guangxiao monastery in Yangzhou, attendant and one of the few recorded dharma-heirs of the great Zhaozhou Congshen. Nicknamed 'Jue Iron-Mouth', he was well versed in the classics and an able poet; a single heir, Daoyan, is recorded under him.
