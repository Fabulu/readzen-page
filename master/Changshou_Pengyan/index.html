Changshou Pengyan 長壽朋彥

School: Fayan | d. 961 | Teacher: Tiantai Deshao

Fayan-school master, abbot of the Changshou cloister at Anguo monastery in Suzhou; a native of Yongjia (秦 clan). One of Tiantai Deshao's forty-nine heirs.
