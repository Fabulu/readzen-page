Baoyue Zhiying 百丈智映

School: Yunmen | Teacher: Zhimen Guangzuo

Song Yunmen-school master of Mount Baizhang in Hongzhou, titled Baoyue (寶月); an heir of Zhimen Guangzuo. The lamps record a monk asking him whose song he sings and whose lineage he succeeds to. The 續傳燈錄 index credits him with two heirs of his own.

References: DILA Authority A021003
